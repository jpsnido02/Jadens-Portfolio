Thought Atlas — initial generated illustration (not rendered in the current interface)
Tool: built-in image_gen; transparent PNG.
Local asset: public/projects/thought-atlas/brain.png
Final prompt:
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: isolated 3D illustration for a personal knowledge-organizing app concept. A beautiful anatomically recognizable human brain with two hemispheres, visible rounded organic gyri and sulci, no face or skull. Three-quarter side/top view, front to left, organized into six large smoothly fitted lobes in muted but saturated coral, buttery yellow, periwinkle blue, peach, seafoam, and lavender. Elegant tactile matte ceramic/clay material with soft ambient occlusion in the folds, fine subtle surface texture, editorial product render, crisp high quality. Each lobe has naturally believable brain folds and softly separated boundary seams, realistic overall brain proportions, NOT a bulbous cartoon cloud and not a pile of balls. Centered full object, generous transparent margin, soft studio light from upper left, fully transparent background, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no labels, no text, no diagram arrows.

Build View — initial generated illustration (not rendered in the current interface)
Tool: built-in image_gen; transparent PNG.
Local asset: public/projects/build-view/xwing.png
Final prompt:
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: isolated 3D object render for a static concept of a spatial LEGO-style building manual. A convincing small brick-built Star Wars X-wing starfighter toy, unmistakably constructed from interlocking plastic toy bricks with clearly visible round studs, seams, plates and small Lego-like parts. White and light gray body, restrained dark red stripe details, transparent smoky cockpit, four splayed wings in X configuration and four long laser cannons, compact slender pointed nose. Highly polished CAD/product render of the assembled miniature, high three-quarter isometric view, nose points down-left, rear wings extend up-right, full object entirely inside the image. Soft studio lighting with tactile ABS plastic sheen, realistic bevels, clean mechanical geometry, precise attractive details. No minifigures, no branding or logos, no stand or hands. Isolated on a fully transparent background with generous margins, no shadow or ground plane. No UI, no labels, no text, no charts, no extra separate pieces.
