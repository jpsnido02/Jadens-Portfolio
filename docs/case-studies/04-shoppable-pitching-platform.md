# Shoppable demo builder

**Shape** Reframe · **Title register** plain name · **Opens** contained ·
**Figures** unnumbered · **Ends** SplitOutcome

Confirmed Reframe. The brief: sales needed live demo prototypes to pitch
shoppable ads, the newest vertical, and design hand-built each one in Figma at
roughly a week apiece. The tool was written in Cursor by the designer who
noticed, and it removed the week entirely.

The hinge is unusually strong because the reframe turns on the author's own
team: the question was never *how do we build prototypes faster*, it was *why
is design in this loop at all*.

## Abstract — ≤45 words
> What the platform is and who used it. Say plainly that it is internal tooling;
> sales-facing work is undersold in portfolios and does not need dressing up.

## Insight — the thing you found
> Comes *before* the thesis in this shape. Here: a week of hand-building per
> prototype, every pitch queued behind design, and a new vertical that could
> not wait for it.

## Thesis — the reframe, one sentence, ≤20 words
> The most useful thing design could do for this vertical was take itself out
> of the loop. Held back until after the insight so the turn lands.

## SplitOutcome
> Two headed lists, Yiko's form. Keep them genuinely different — if the second
> list restates the first, the split is decoration.
>
> **Business result** — what the company got. Pitch cycle time, win rate, deal
> size, adoption by the sales team.
>
> **Design contribution** — what design specifically moved. Alignment on what a
> shoppable ad even is, a reusable component set, a shared vocabulary.

## Takeaway ×2
> One on internal tools as product, one on designing for a sales motion.
