# McDonald's in-person advertisements on kiosk

**Shape** Iteration · **Title register** thesis · **Opens** full bleed ·
**Figures** numbered · **Ends** Numbers

The strongest Iteration candidate in the set, because its constraints are unlike
anything else in the portfolio and will have produced genuine failures. Also the
most memorable project here, which is why it opens on a photograph and is the
only page that earns an `Appendix`.

---

## Masthead
- `year`
- `client` McDonald's — fallback: *a global QSR chain*
- `role`
- `duration`

## Abstract — ≤45 words
> Where this lives: a physical kiosk, in a restaurant, mid-order. Establish
> immediately that this is not a web placement, because that is the whole
> interest of the project.

## Thesis — one sentence, ≤20 words
> What changes when the ad is in person. The honest version is probably about
> the audience being physically present and in a hurry, with other people
> watching.

## Figure 1 — full bleed, kiosk in situ
> A real photograph of the real thing in a real restaurant. Not a mockup. This
> single image does more than any amount of prose about physical context.
> Caption ≤25 words.

## Criteria — the constraint set, 01 / 02 / 03
> TikTok's numbered form: a number, one word, and a question. The constraints
> are the content here, so this block does heavy lifting.
>
> - `01 Dwell` — how many seconds does the screen actually have?
> - `02 Privacy` — who else can see this screen?
> - `03 Input` — what can someone do with greasy hands and no personal device?
>
> Replace with the real three. Each can carry a figure.

## Quote

## Numbers — 2–3 metrics
> In-person engagement numbers are unusual and therefore interesting even if
> they are modest. Give the denominator.

## Takeaway ×2
> One about designing for physical space, one about constraints as a brief.
> ≤50 words each.

## Appendix — the only one in the set
> The outtake. The odd thing someone did at the kiosk, the thing that broke in
> store, the reaction nobody predicted. Alvin ends on his dad trying to scrub
> video with his head and someone using a knife. Ending on a human detail beats
> ending on a metric, and this is the project that has one.
