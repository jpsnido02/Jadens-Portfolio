# Macy's Bag page optimization

**Shape** Iteration · **Title register** thesis · **Opens** before/after pair ·
**Figures** numbered · **Ends** Numbers

Leads the portfolio because it is the one with real numbers. The persuasive
content is the variants that lost — three `Attempt` blocks, then a winner traced
to a specific observation rather than asserted.

---

## Masthead
- `year`
- `client` Macy's — fallback if naming is restricted: *a national department store*
- `role`
- `duration`

## Abstract — ≤45 words
> What the bag page is, and what was wrong with it. Name the stake in money or
> intent, not in adjectives. No "seamless", no "delightful".

## Thesis — one sentence, ≤20 words
> The argument the project made. Something along the lines of: the bag page is
> the highest-intent moment on the site and the worst-defended. Write the
> version that is true.

## Confidential — one line
> What is simplified, redacted or recreated here, and why. Covers you and reads
> as professionalism rather than hedging.

## Figure 1 — pair, before / after
> Caption ≤25 words. Say what changed, not that it improved.

## Attempt 1 — label in caps
> `ATTEMPT 1: <what you tried>`
>
> One sentence on the idea. One sentence on what broke. Name the mechanism —
> "it pushed the subtotal below the fold on 375px", not "it tested poorly".
> Figure optional.

## Attempt 2 — label in caps
> `ATTEMPT 2: <what you tried>`
>
> Same structure. A different *kind* of failure from Attempt 1 — if both failed
> for the same reason, you only have one attempt.

## Attempt 3 — the winner
> `<WHAT WON>`
>
> Open with the observation that unlocked it, from research or from the data.
> Alvin's version: a participant held down arrow keys to scrub YouTube. Then
> three short reasons it was chosen. This is the most important block on the
> page — the solution has to be *earned* here, not announced.

## Quote — one per case study, maximum
> Pull quote from a partner, PM or stakeholder. Name and role attributed.
> Skip the block entirely rather than use a weak quote.

## Numbers — 2–3 metrics
> Figure, then label. Include the baseline or the window if the number needs it
> to be honest.

## Takeaway ×2 — opinionated titles
> `Takeaway: <a claim, not a topic>`
>
> "in praise of the barely working prototype" over "Learnings". ≤50 words each.
