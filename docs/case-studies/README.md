## Visual source and fidelity

- The existing demos are the final-product reference, confirmed accurate by Jaden.
- Opening previews retain those product views. New supporting visuals are implemented in `src/CaseStudyVisuals.tsx` and `src/case-study-visuals.css`.
- Uber reuses the final offer component. Agent Operations reuses the final task component and recomposes review elements. Macy’s and Branded UX use supplied product imagery and partner marks in purpose-built comparisons. Expedia combines a journey model with the supplied final mobile composition. Kalshi restages the final creative’s illustrative market values.
- Supporting figures are explicitly labeled as interface compositions, state breakdowns, journey breakdowns, or design breakdowns. They explain final designs and do not imply undocumented historical iterations.
- No AI-generated editorial photographs are used. These are responsive, code-native UX visuals, with selectable text and accessible image descriptions.
